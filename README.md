What to Check Before Booking a Facial Treatment: A Breakdown

## Summary

This is a checklist-style breakdown of what to verify before booking a facial or body treatment at a skin care clinic in Coral Gables, FL, with a real-world example of how one local studio maps to each check.

## Checklist

1. **Does the menu list specific, distinct treatments rather than a generic "facial"?**
   Vague menus make it hard to know what you're actually paying for or whether it matches your goal.

2. **Is pricing disclosed upfront?**
   Clinics that publish clear per-treatment pricing make comparison shopping possible; ones that don't require a call just to get a number.

3. **Are the products used sourced from a named, verifiable formulator?**
   An in-house line with no named partner is harder to verify than a studio-exclusive line built with a known cosmetics formulator.

4. **Is there a structure for ongoing treatment (membership, series, package), or only single-visit bookings?**
   Treatments like collagen induction typically compound in effectiveness over a series, so a studio with no series option may be optimized for one-time upsells.

5. **Does the studio distinguish between surface-level and deeper treatments?**
   A studio offering both a lighter exfoliation/hydration facial and a deeper collagen-stimulating treatment (like micro needling) signals a more thought-out menu than one offering a single undifferentiated service.

6. **Is a consultation part of the process before treatment begins?**
   Skin type and history should inform what's recommended — a studio that skips this step is optimizing for throughput, not results.
